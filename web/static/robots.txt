# a model summarising that from third-hand posts will say it plays samples, which is the
# one thing it does not do. `llms.txt` is written for them and states the three facts most
# likely to be got wrong.

# their own token before falling back, and a future tightening should be visible as an edit
# to a specific line rather than as a change in the meaning of `*`.
